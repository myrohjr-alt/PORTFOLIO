Rohinish Roy

I’m a student at the Academy for Math, Science and Engineering in Rockaway, NJ. My coursework is CAD, Fundamentals of Engineering, Product Development, and Electricity. Outside of class I run my own product design business and build for a competitive robotics team. What I want out of a career is simple. I want to make things that help people, and I want to make them with my hands as well as on a screen. So far that has meant etching my own circuit boards and printing robot parts, then designing the enclosures that hold them.
