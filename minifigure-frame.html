Minifigure Picture Frame

One block of spruce, milled to hold six minifigures

Role: Solo build Year: 2026

The body is one block of Sitka spruce. I cut six pockets into it on a vertical mill, sized so a minifigure sits in each one.

A laser cut acrylic pane covers each pocket. Behind the figure sits an MDF disc, held by PLA blocks I printed and MDF tabs cut on the same laser. It hangs on keyhole slots cut into the back.

Body
Sitka spruce, CNC machined on a vertical mill
Panes
Laser cut acrylic, six off
Backing
MDF discs with PLA mounts and laser cut MDF tabs
Hardware
PLA leveler and pin-hole blocks, ST2.9 × 6.5 stainless screws
Capacity
Six minifigures
Drawings
Four-sheet set with full part and assembly views
