Flora

A flowerpot that waters itself, ringed by picture frames

Role: CAD, printing, and laser cutting Year: 2025

My team built Flora for the Technology Student Association’s Manufacturing Prototype competition. The prompt asked for a picture frame that did a second job. We made a flower pot that waters itself and wrapped picture frames around the outside.

I drew the full CAD and the technical drawings for the pot and the frames in Onshape. I printed the pot on my Bambu Lab A1 in PETG, using custom settings to keep it watertight, and cut the frames on my school’s laser cutter. I helped with the circuit too. I picked the parts and wrote the Arduino UNO code that runs the pump, so the pot waters itself on a timer you set with a potentiometer.
