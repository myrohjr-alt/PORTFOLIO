Darksaber

A 32.55-inch Star Wars prop with a three-layer blade

Role: Solo build Year: 2025

This is a Darksaber from Star Wars. The hilt is PETG, printed on an FDM machine. The blade is laser cut.

The blade is where the real decision was. It is not one cut part. It is three layers: an acrylic core running the full length, with an MDF face on each side. Acrylic on its own is stiff but brittle and would snap. MDF on its own would flex across a 32.55 inch span. Stacked, the acrylic carries the load and the MDF gives the blade its faces.
